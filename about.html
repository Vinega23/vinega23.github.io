Our Story

Cloudberry Escapes creates bespoke journeys in Northern Norway, combining raw Arctic nature with refined Arctic luxury. Founded by Bram Bril and Yves Jacobs, we specialize in tailor-made travel experiences designed for guests seeking authenticity, comfort, and seamless execution.


Our journeys go beyond standard itineraries, offering rare access to the landscapes, culture, and hidden corners of the Arctic. Inspired by the cloudberry — viddas gull, the “gold of the plateaus” — our approach is rooted in exclusivity, local knowledge, and deep respect for the region.


From full services hotels and lodges to private properties and villas to carefully curated Arctic experiences, every detail is thoughtfully crafted to reflect the true spirit of the north. With trusted local partners and hands-on expertise, we ensure each journey is effortless, personal, and exceptional.

Meet the Team

Founder & Chief Experience Officer

Yves Jacobs

Yves Jacobs is a multilingual Arctic travel specialist with over a decade of experience across Northern Europe. With a background in operations, product development, and bespoke guest journeys, he brings deep regional knowledge and a hands-on approach to creating seamless, high-quality experiences in remote Arctic destinations.

Founder & Head of Sales

Bram Bril

Bram Bril is an experienced tourism professional based in Alta, Northern Norway, with over a decade of experience in Arctic travel and destination development. With a background spanning sales, business development, and guest experience, he combines international perspective with deep local knowledge to create thoughtful, high-quality Arctic journeys.
