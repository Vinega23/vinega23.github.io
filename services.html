Our Services

At Cloudberry Escapes, we craft seamless journeys that capture the essence of Northern Norway’s beauty and culture. Our suite of services ensures every aspect of your trip is thoughtfully designed and effortlessly executed.

Local Expertise

With deep knowledge and local connections, we offer authentic experiences that only insiders can provide.

Local Expertise

With co-founder Yves Jacobs’ background in Arctic tourism operations and destination management, every journey is designed with seamless execution in mind. Meanwhile, Bram Bril’s deep experience in luxury travel sales ensures that each itinerary is crafted to meet the refined expectations of our discerning clientele. Together, they bring a level of precision, authenticity, and exclusivity that simply can’t be found elsewhere.

Off The Beaten Track

Escape the crowds. We specialize in curating itineraries into wild, untouched landscapes where nature reigns supreme and privacy is guaranteed.

Hidden Gems

Our scouts are constantly exploring Northern Norway to find locations that haven't made it to the guidebooks yet. We take you to secluded fjords, private islands, and mountain cabins accessible only by helicopter or private boat. Experience the silence of the Arctic in locations where you are the only guests for miles.

Unforgettable Experiences

From dog sledding across frozen lakes to whale watching under the midnight sun, we craft moments that linger in memory forever.

Immersive Activities

Whether it's a private Sami cultural encounter, a King Crab safari, or hunting for the Northern Lights with a professional photographer, we curate activities that resonate with your interests. We work exclusively with top-tier local providers to ensure safety, comfort, and a truly magical experience.

Travel Concierge

Our full-service concierge handles every detail, from restaurant reservations to activity bookings, ensuring your Arctic escape is effortless.

24/7 Support

Relax knowing that we are always just a phone call away. Need to change a dinner reservation? Want to add a last-minute spa treatment? Our concierge team is dedicated to fulfilling your requests swiftly and discreetly, allowing you to focus entirely on enjoying your journey.

Private Transport

Navigate the Arctic in safety and style. We provide high-end private transportation with experienced drivers, so you can relax and enjoy the scenery.

Journey in Comfort

Our fleet consists of premium 4x4 vehicles equipped to handle Arctic winter conditions with ease. Our drivers are local experts who know the roads intimately. For longer distances or a truly spectacular arrival, we can also arrange private helicopter transfers or chartered yachts.

Ready to Discover Your Arctic Adventure?

Let us craft your perfect Northern Norway escape. Reach out to our team to begin the conversation.
