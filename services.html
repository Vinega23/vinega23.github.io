Our Services

At Cloudberry Escapes, we craft seamless journeys that capture the essence of Northern Norway’s beauty and culture. Our suite of services ensures every aspect of your trip is thoughtfully designed and effortlessly executed.

Local Expertise

Rooted in the heart of the Arctic, our team possesses an intimate knowledge of Northern Norway's hidden gems. We move beyond the guidebooks, offering you access to secluded fjords, private dining experiences in Sami lavvus, and the best vantage points for the Northern Lights.

Our network of local partners ensures that every interaction is authentic, supporting the community while providing you with an unparalleled depth of cultural immersion.

Off The Beaten Track

Escape the crowds and discover the silence of the true North. We specialize in curating itineraries that take you into the wild, untouched landscapes where nature reigns supreme. Whether it’s a snowshoe trek through silent pine forests or a private boat charter to uninhabited islands, we ensure privacy and exclusivity.

Adventure lies where the road ends. Let us guide you to the places that remain pristine and majestic, far removed from standard tourist trails.

Unforgettable Experiences

From the adrenaline rush of dog sledding across frozen lakes to the serene majesty of whale watching under the midnight sun, we craft moments that linger in memory forever. Our experiences are tailored to your pace and preferences, ensuring a perfect balance of thrill and relaxation.

We curate every detail, from thermal suits to gourmet wilderness lunches, so you can focus entirely on the magic of the moment.

Travel Concierge

Your comfort is our priority. Our full-service travel concierge handles every logistical detail of your journey. From the moment you land until your departure, we provide 24/7 support, restaurant reservations, activity bookings, and special requests.

Enjoy the luxury of time. We manage the schedule, adapt to weather changes seamlessly, and ensure that your Arctic escape is effortless and stress-free.

Private Transport

Navigate the Arctic in safety and style. We provide high-end private transportation with experienced drivers who are experts in winter driving conditions. Whether you require a luxury SUV for a couple or a spacious van for a group, our fleet is equipped for comfort and reliability.

Door-to-door service means you never have to worry about maps or icy roads. Sit back, relax, and enjoy the stunning scenery rolling past your window.

Let us craft your perfect Northern Norway escape. Reach out to our team to begin the conversation.
